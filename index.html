    How to read the bay

    Wreckd listens to the Pons launch factory on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). Every new token appears as a ship. Each buy fills its sail, each sell lets wind out of it, and when nobody trades for fifteen minutes the ship takes on water and settles on the seabed, where you can still inspect what it carried.

    A ship whose bonding curve holds 4.2 ETH graduates to a DEX and sails off with brass sails. Wallets that buy within three seconds of a launch are drawn as sharks. The weather follows the whole launchpad: a burst of volume brings a storm, a dead hour brings fog. Every ship has a captain, the wallet that launched it, and the captain's dossier shows whether they have launched before and whether they sold their own ship. When tokens are sent to the dead address (0x…dEaD) or burned, fire breaks out on deck; a lantern stays lit at the stern of every ship whose captain burned tokens. The furnace lists every burn with its share of the supply and of the captain's bag, which is what the captain bought on the curve.

    Read-only
    No wallet connection, no keys, no transactions. Everything comes from the public Robinhood Chain RPC.
    Not advice
    A ship afloat is not a recommendation. Most launches sink.
